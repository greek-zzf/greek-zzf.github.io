健康数据如何被读取和处理

你授权的 Apple 健康数据全部只在你的设备上读取和处理，用来导出成 CSV、JSON 或每日 Markdown。开发者没有服务器，不会收集、上传或出售任何数据。

数据会发到哪里

数据只会发到你自己选择的目的地：

  • 「文件」或 iCloud（也可以是你选中的 Obsidian 库文件夹）
  • Google Drive
  • Notion
  • 你填写的 Webhook

这些服务如何保存和使用数据，以它们自己的隐私政策为准。Health Export 不会把数据发到上述目的地以外的地方。

令牌如何保存

Google 和 Notion 的令牌保存在 iOS 钥匙串（Keychain）里，用来访问你已经连接的目的地。

广告、分析与第三方 SDK

本 App 没有广告，没有分析，也不含第三方 SDK。除了你配置的目的地之外，App 不会发起其他网络请求。

HealthKit 与营销

HealthKit 数据不会用于广告或营销。

联系

对这份政策或 App 的数据处理有问题，请发邮件到 felixzhou@mail.grokbot.com。

Health Export · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-12

Who provides this app

Health Export is developed by Felix. Contact: felixzhou@mail.grokbot.com.

How health data is read and processed

All Apple Health data you authorize is read and processed on your device only, so it can be exported as CSV, JSON, or daily Markdown. The developer has no servers and does not collect, upload, or sell any data.

Where data is sent

Data is sent only to destinations you choose:

  • Files or iCloud, including an Obsidian vault folder you select
  • Google Drive
  • Notion
  • A Webhook address you provide

Those services handle the data under their own privacy policies. Health Export does not send data anywhere else.

How tokens are stored

Google and Notion tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain and are used only to reach destinations you have connected.

Advertising, analytics, and third-party SDKs

The app has no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests other than to destinations you configure.

HealthKit and marketing

HealthKit data is never used for advertising or marketing.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about how the app handles data: felixzhou@mail.grokbot.com.
